# Actionability cold derivation — d5

Derived only from the narration payload. Nothing is assumed to exist already.

## Cross-cutting foundation (used by every scene)

- **Models:**
  - `Program(id, name, buyer_of_record_org FK Org)`
  - `Org(id, name)`
  - `ProgramMembership(user FK, program FK)`
  - `Supplier(id, org FK, name)`
  - `Tender(id, program FK, round_number, product, quantity, unit, delivery_point, deadline, status[draft|open|closed|awarded|in_transit|delivered|paid], published bool)`
  - `Quote(id, tender FK, supplier FK, unit_price, currency, freight_terms[included|excluded|not_specified], incoterm, lead_time_days, is_current bool, supersedes FK self null, voided bool)`
  - `Shipment(id, tender FK, expected_arrival date, status)`
  - `Payment(id, tender FK, amount, paid_on)`
  - `Outreach(id, tender FK, supplier FK, sent_at, replied_at null)`
  - `Award(id, tender FK, quote FK, reason text, provisional bool, awarded_by FK User, awarded_at)`
- **Revision / provenance table:** `Revision(id, program FK, object_type, object_id, field, old_value JSON, new_value JSON, summary text, actor_user FK null, actor_kind[human|agent], actor_label, channel[web|mcp], source_excerpt text null, idempotency_key char unique null, recorded_at, effective_at)`. Every write goes through one `record_change()` service, which writes the model and appends a `Revision`.
- **Idempotency:** `record_change(..., idempotency_key=sha256(source_message_id + field + new_value))`. On a duplicate key it returns the existing revision and writes nothing.
- **As-of reconstruction:** `state_as_of(program, date)` replays or rewinds `Revision` rows whose `effective_at <= date`. An object whose creation revision is after the date is excluded.
- **Seed command:** `manage.py seed_sophie_rutf`. It is idempotent and creates:
  - program "Connect-RUTF", with buyer of record Org "Dimagi", and user Sophie as a member;
  - round 1: quote → award → shipment. An ETA revision 5 Sep → 19 Sep is recorded twice with the same email id, so only one row lands. Then payment;
  - round 2, open, with 3 quotes, all with a missing basis. One is AI-entered with `freight_terms=not_specified`, and its excerpt states "freight included";
  - outreach to 2 suppliers with `sent_at` 15 days ago and no reply;
  - ≥2 other programs with open published tenders;
  - rewind anchors, so that on 20 Aug round 1 is `in_transit` with ETA 5 Sep and round 2 does not yet exist.
- **Test:** after `seed_sophie_rutf` runs twice, `Revision.objects.filter(summary__contains="ETA").count() == 1`.

## Scene 1: Sophie reads where her program stands today

- **Route:** `GET /supply/` resolves the user's current program (or `/supply/?program=<id>`). View `supply_overview`, template `supply/overview.html`.
- **Header:** `data-testid="supply-header"` renders `"{program.name} · {buyer_of_record.name}, buyer of record"`.
  - Test: the response contains `"Connect-RUTF · Dimagi, buyer of record"`.
- **Scoping:** the queryset is `Tender.objects.filter(program=program)` only.
  - Test: a tender from another program is absent from `response.context["rows"]`.
- **Rows:** one row per tender or order, `data-testid="overview-row-{tender.id}"`, with these cells:
  - `stage` (status label);
  - `waiting_on` (e.g. "waiting on quotes", computed from status and open outreach);
  - `last_changed_at`, `last_changed_by`, taken from the latest `Revision` for that object and its children.
  - Test: the round 1 row shows "delivered" and "paid". The round 2 row shows "open" and "waiting on quotes".
- **Stale flag:** `data-testid="stale-flag"` appears when an `Outreach` has `replied_at is null` and `sent_at < now-14d`, or a current `Quote` has a missing basis. The reasons appear in the flag's title.
  - Test: the round 2 row contains `stale-flag` and the round 1 row does not.
- **AI badge:** `data-testid="ai-badge"` appears when the latest revision has `channel == "mcp"`.
  - Test: the badge is present on the seeded round 2 row, whose last write was over MCP, and absent on rows whose last write came from the web.
- **Service:** `overview_rows(program, as_of=None)` returns dataclasses. Unit test: 2 rows for the seeded program.

## Scene 2: Sophie finds the day round 1's arrival slipped

- **Route:** `GET /supply/tenders/<id>/` renders the tender detail. It shows the current state card (`data-testid="tender-state"`) and then the timeline `data-testid="timeline"`.
- **Timeline:** `timeline_for(tender)` covers revisions on the tender and its quotes, award, shipment and payment, ordered `-recorded_at`. Each item is `data-testid="timeline-item"` and shows the plain sentence `summary`.
  - Test: the first item concerns payment and the last concerns the first quote.
- **ETA sentence:** the summary is formatted `"ETA {old:%-d %b} → {new:%-d %b}"`.
  - Test: exactly one item has the text `"ETA 5 Sep → 19 Sep"`.
- **Actor badge:** `data-testid="actor-badge"` shows `actor_label`, and `"ACE (agent)"` when `actor_kind == agent`. Agent badges are a `<button>` that toggles `data-testid="source-excerpt"` containing `source_excerpt`.
  - Test: the ETA item's badge text is "ACE (agent)" and its excerpt is non-empty in the DOM.
- **Web-entered lines:** these show `actor_user.get_full_name()` and render no `source-excerpt` element.
  - Test: an item by Sophie has no `[data-testid=source-excerpt]`.
- **Dedup:** the MCP tool `supply_shipment_update(shipment_id, expected_arrival, source_message_id, excerpt)` passes an idempotency key.
  - Test: calling it twice with the same `source_message_id` leaves one `Revision` and returns `{"deduplicated": true}` the second time.
- **Access:** only program members may view the page.
  - Test: a non-member gets 404.

## Scene 3: Sophie looks at her program as it stood on 20 Aug

- **Date control:** the header has a date control `<input type="date" name="as_of" data-testid="as-of-picker">` in a GET form. It submits to `/supply/?as_of=2026-08-20`.
- **As-of view:** when `as_of` is present, the view calls `overview_rows(program, as_of=date)` using `state_as_of`.
- **Banner:** `data-testid="as-of-banner"` reads `"Viewing as of 20 Aug 2026 — back to today"`. The `back to today` link has `data-testid="back-to-today"` and `href="/supply/"`.
  - Test: the banner text is present, and the link's href is `/supply/`.
- **Read-only:** write controls (new tender, award, correct and similar buttons carrying `data-write-control`) are suppressed when `as_of` is set. POST handlers reject a request carrying `as_of` with 400.
  - Test: `data-write-control` does not appear in the as-of response.
- **Content:** the round 1 row shows stage "in transit" and expected arrival "5 Sep". There is no round 2 row, because its creation revision is after 20 Aug.
  - Test: `rows` has length 1, and `rows[0].expected_arrival == date(2026,9,5)`.
- **Return:** `GET /supply/` (no param) shows the current overview again.
  - Test: 2 rows and no banner.
- **Validation:** a future or malformed `as_of` gets a 400, or is ignored and a message is shown.

## Scene 4: Sophie reads what each round 2 quote is missing

- **Route:** `GET /supply/tenders/<id>/compare/` renders `supply/compare.html`.
- **Comparability service:** `compare(tender)` returns `{comparable: [...], needs_info: [...], summary}`. A quote needs info if any required basis field (`freight_terms`, `incoterm`, `currency`, `unit` / pack size, `lead_time_days`) is null or `not_specified`.
- **Banner:** `data-testid="compare-banner"` reads "None of the 3 quotes can be compared yet" when `comparable` is empty.
  - Test: the exact text is present for seeded round 2.
- **Needs info section:** `data-testid="needs-info"` lists every quote as `data-testid="needs-info-quote-{id}"`. Each shows:
  - the missing basis in words (e.g. "Missing: freight terms (included or excluded)"), from a `BASIS_LABELS` map;
  - who can answer it (e.g. "Ask: {supplier.contact_name}, {supplier.name}"), from a `Supplier.contact_name` field.
  - Test: 3 items. Each has non-empty `.missing-basis` and `.who-can-answer`, and none shows the raw field name.
- **AI badge:** a quote whose creating revision has `channel == mcp` shows `data-testid="ai-badge"`.
  - Test: the badge count equals the number of MCP-created seeded quotes.

## Scene 5: Sophie checks one AI-entered quote against the email and corrects it

- **Route:** `GET /supply/quotes/<id>/` renders quote detail and the quote timeline, reusing the `timeline` partial.
- **Creation sentence:** the creating revision's summary is `"Quote recorded … (freight: not specified)"`. It is badged as AI (`actor-badge` = "ACE (agent)"), and its expandable `source-excerpt` contains the supplier email text stating freight terms.
  - Test: the excerpt contains "freight".
- **Actions on agent lines:** for program members, a line with `actor_kind == agent` shows `data-testid="correct-btn"` and `data-testid="void-btn"`.
  - Test: both are present on the AI line, and absent for a non-member and in as-of mode.
- **Correct form:** `GET/POST /supply/quotes/<id>/correct/` uses a `QuoteCorrectionForm`. It holds the quote's fields prefilled (`freight_terms` select with the choice `included`), plus a required `reason` textarea (`data-testid="correction-reason"`).
- **On save:** the server creates a new `Quote` with `supersedes=old` and `is_current=True`, and sets `old.is_current=False`. It writes a `Revision(actor_user=request.user, actor_kind=human, channel=web, summary="Corrected freight: not specified → included — {reason}")`, then redirects to `/supply/quotes/<new_id>/`.
  - Test: POST returns 302 to the new id. `new.freight_terms == "included"`, `old.is_current is False`, and a missing reason re-renders the form with an error.
- **Timeline order:** the new quote's timeline includes its predecessor's revisions. The correction is on top, badged "Sophie", with the AI's original entry below.
  - Test: `items[0].actor_label == "Sophie"`, and a later item is the AI "Quote recorded" line.
- **Void:** `POST /supply/quotes/<id>/void/` requires a reason, sets `voided=True` and adds a revision.
  - Test: a voided quote is excluded from `compare()`.

## Scene 6: Sophie sees what other buyers and suppliers can see of round 2

- **Market list:** `GET /supply/market/` lists all `Tender.objects.filter(status="open", published=True)` across programs as `data-testid="market-tender-{id}"`, with the buyer org name.
  - Test: the seeded round 2 is present plus at least 2 tenders from other programs.
- **Market detail:** `GET /supply/market/tenders/<id>/` has its own template, `market_tender.html`. It renders only the published fields `product`, `quantity`, `delivery_point` and `deadline` (`data-testid="published-fields"`).
- **What it hides:** the template does not include the timeline, source excerpts or quotes. The view's context carries no revisions or quotes (a serializer whitelist).
  - Test: the response has no `data-testid="timeline"`, no `source-excerpt`, and no quote price or supplier name text. This holds even for Sophie (a member) viewing the market page.
- **Permission:** revision and excerpt endpoints (`/supply/tenders/<id>/`, `/supply/quotes/<id>/`) check `ProgramMembership`.
  - Test: a user from another program gets 404 on the round 2 tender detail and 200 on its market page.
- **Link to history:** members see a link "View history (program members only)" pointing to the tender detail. Non-members do not.

## Scene 7: Sophie awards round 2 and says why she will not wait

- **Ranking:** `compare()` ranks comparable quotes by landed unit price. After the correction in Scene 5, the corrected quote moves to `comparable` and appears as `data-testid="ranked-quote-{id}"`.
- **Provisional marker:** `provisional = len(needs_info) > 0`, shown as `data-testid="provisional-marker"` with "provisional — 2 quotes still blocked".
  - Test: after the correction, `compare()` returns 1 comparable and 2 needs_info, and provisional is true.
- **Award form in the row:** each row has an award form: `reason` textarea (`data-testid="award-reason"`, required) and `data-testid="award-btn"`. It POSTs to `/supply/tenders/<id>/award/` with `quote_id` and `reason`.
- **Award server behaviour:**
  - creates `Award(quote, reason, provisional=True, awarded_by=request.user)`;
  - sets `tender.status="awarded"`;
  - writes `Revision(actor_user=Sophie, channel=web, summary="Awarded to {supplier} (provisional) — {reason}")`;
  - redirects to `/supply/`.
  - Test: `Award.objects.get(tender=round2).provisional is True`, and an empty reason gives a form error with no award.
- **Guards:** awarding a voided or superseded quote is rejected with 400, as is awarding while in as-of mode or as a non-member.
- **Overview after award:** the round 2 row reads "awarded, provisional", with `last_changed_at` date == today and `last_changed_by == "Sophie"`.
  - Test: after the POST, GET `/supply/` shows the round 2 row text containing "awarded", "provisional", "Sophie" and today's date. The stale flag no longer blocks.
