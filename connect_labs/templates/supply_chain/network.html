Why a store can be "ok" while its branch is not

Two readings can look like they disagree. On its own shelf and its status chip (ok, low) are about the store itself. With everything below it adds the stores and field workers it supplies, so a store can be "ok" on its own shelf while the whole branch beneath it is "overstocked".
