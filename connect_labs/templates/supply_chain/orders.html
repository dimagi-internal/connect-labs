Why choosing and ordering are separate

Choosing a supplier and placing the order are separate steps. The organisation that chooses isn't always the one that buys. For example, you pick the supplier and a local partner places and pays for the order. Each order records who bought it, because import duty and tax fall on whoever brings the goods in.
