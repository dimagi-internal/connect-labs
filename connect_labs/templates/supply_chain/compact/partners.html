Each local partner (LLO) runs its own network and says how it gets stock to its field workers (FLWs). Its card is its home: who to write to, how it distributes, its places and who looks after each, and a line on how it stands today.

Network, stock, issues and alerts are then shown LLO by LLO, with a roll-up across all of them.

Award emails are addressed to an LLO's main contact. To add the first LLO, state how it distributes; its network is then set up.
