Eighteen figures a chain, most of them nought, every one of them the same size as every other. It answered "where is each decision up to" in a space the reader was using to ask "what is going on". The decisions are not the lead story; understanding the situation is. So the lead is now where the goods ARE, what is odd about that, and what is owed -- and the lifecycle counts sit underneath, behind a disclosure, intact and one click away. Nothing was removed from the page; what changed is which question it answers first. Nothing here is summed across programmes and nothing is ranked between them.
