Extends the ROOT base for the reason portfolio.html gives: the supply nav is one program's, and this page spans several. Three levels, one panel: the portfolio (a card per program, its chain as three stages), a program (what is in the way at each stage, its orders and places), and a place. The map follows the panel: it fits to what is being read, and draws only what that level needs -- stores coloured by one thing (blocked / waiting on an answer / nothing open), and the routes supplies are on their way along. Everything is in `map_payload` (portfolio/map_data.py); the script filters in the browser. What is counted is PLACES and ORDERS, never a quantity across programs. A place with no location and a blocker with no place are listed in the panel, never dropped.
