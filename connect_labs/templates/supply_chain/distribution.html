The chain, in order: stock reaches a store or facility (deliveries), then goes out to field workers in a weekly handout. A handout is planned first, then confirmed with what was actually given (from a WhatsApp message, a photo of the paper list, or one button), and the person who received it can confirm too, so each handover is recorded from both sides. Only confirmed amounts move stock.
