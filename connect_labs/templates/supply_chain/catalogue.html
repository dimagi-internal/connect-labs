Why a product, a trade item and a batch are three different things

A product is a specification — "RUTF, 92 g sachet, 150 to the carton" — and nobody can ship you one. A trade item is one manufacturer's product against that specification, with its own SKU and GTIN, and is what you actually order. A batch is the lot that arrived, and carries the expiry and the certificate; it appears under Stock. So every supplier's RUTF is the same 92 g sachet because the product says so and each trade item is checked against it — while their SKUs stay their own. Conflating any two is how a per‑sachet figure silently goes wrong.
