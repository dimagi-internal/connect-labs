How a test is judged

A batch test or a dispenser sample is held to the product's own specification in the Catalogue: each figure must be measured and met to pass. The average of the trials is worked out for you. A storage check fails on any “No”. A failed batch, dispenser or store is shown at the top, and on the Overview, until a newer test of the same one passes. The list shows what was measured; each test's result is in the download. A test recorded by mistake is withdrawn with a reason, never deleted.
