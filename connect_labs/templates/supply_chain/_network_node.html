, so a store with two hundred of them stays one row until opened. Written for someone reading stock, not keeping the directory: the row leads with what the point holds (`node.stock`, a `network_tree` row) -- on hand, cover and its band -- and a store's next line with the same for everything below it plus how many workers are below their minimum. The directory facts (slug, Connect user, place, the band set on it, how it is known, what the dispensing rests on, what came in from outside) sit behind "Details". A store's own figures are what is AT it; "with everything below it" sums on hand and what came in from outside the subtree (each unit once, never a hop-summed "issued"). Cover is recomputed for the subtree, never summed. A store that passes stock on (holds nothing itself while the points below it hold some -- `passes_stock_on`) says so in grey: its empty shelf is how it works, not a stock-out. The red alarm stays for points meant to hold stock. {% endcomment %}
