{% comment %} The tabs on every page of the cost-effectiveness explorer. `active` is "indicators", "models", "implementation" or "market". Health indicators = what the baseline says about a place. CEA models = run a Connect model, change its inputs, test sensitivity. Implementations = what delivered opportunities achieved, at what they cost. Market size = what it would cost to reach everyone in chosen places, and where the models say it is worth it. {% endcomment %}
