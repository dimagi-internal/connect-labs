What is this based on?

Mortality — DHS subnational estimates where a country has a survey, otherwise the UN IGME national figure applied to its regions. Rows say which.

Population — WorldPop 100 m age–sex grids (CC BY 4.0), aggregated over each boundary by WorldPop's own zonal-statistics service.

Births — derived: the under-1 population divided by infant survivorship. Not measured; the formula travels with the download.

Boundaries — geoBoundaries ADM0/ADM1 (CC BY 4.0).

Mortality is measured at ADM1 at best. Nothing here implies sub-regional mortality.
