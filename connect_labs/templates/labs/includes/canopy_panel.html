A page opts in by putting `canopy_panel` in its context and including this at the end of its `javascript` block: context["canopy_panel"] = canopy.panel_context( resource="labs-marketplace://orgs", backing_tool="marketplace_orgs_get", visible_ids=[row["org"].slug for row in listed], filters=state["selected"], ) {% include "labs/includes/canopy_panel.html" %} `visible_ids` is the SELECTION — slugs or ids — never the rows. The agent reads the rows itself through `backing_tool`, live, so one place decides what a row says and one place decides who may read it. Canopy refuses a page state over 8 KiB: generous for several hundred ids, deliberately too small for the rows behind them. Nothing renders unless the deployment holds a signing key, because a launcher that opens onto an error the page cannot explain is worse than no launcher.
