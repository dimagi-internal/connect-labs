Buildings reached

For each facility, the buildings around it are fixed first (its nearest buildings, from the microplanner's own footprints). A building counts as reached in a cycle when a household visited that cycle has its recorded GPS point within the chosen distance of it. Nothing here shows a household or a point.

Form paths (defaults are the eCHIS Niger SMC form)

Read it carefully. The share depends on the distance you pick and on how dense the buildings are, so compare a district with itself over cycles, or districts at one distance, not districts with different densities against each other.

Households with no usable point (none recorded, 0,0, far from the facility, or poor accuracy) cannot be placed, so buildings reached is a floor. Each is counted by kind below, none is silently dropped.

A cycle's headline share is out of the buildings of the facilities that ran that cycle.
