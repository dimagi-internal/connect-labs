Buildings reached

Of the buildings around each facility, the share that visited households' GPS points came within the chosen distance of, by cycle.

Data:

Read in memory for this request as you, and never saved. Only counts and 1 km squares come back, never a household or a point.

What to export, and a template

One row per household and cycle with these columns: facility_id, household_id, lat, lon, cycle, children_served, and optionally accuracy_m. facility_id must match the ids in the facility list below; cycle is any label ending in the cycle number (1, cycle 2, contact_3); children_served is how many children the household's records show given the dose in that cycle (0 if visited and none served); lat, lon is the household's recorded point. Rows with no usable point still count as households but cannot be placed.

From CommCare HQ: export the distribution form (one row per child) with the household case id, the household's point, the facility and the passage; then count the children who received the dose per household and passage (in pandas: df[df.dose=="oui"].groupby(["facility_id","household_id","lat","lon","cycle"]).size().reset_index(name="children_served")), and add rows with 0 for visited households where nobody was served.

Distance: how close a household's point must be to a building for it to count as reached. Buildings per facility: each facility's catchment is its nearest this-many buildings.

About the facility list

One facility per line (or separated by ;). id must match the facility value in the data (facility_id in an uploaded file). lon,lat is the facility's location: from the HQ location data, or a public health-facility layer (HDX, OpenStreetMap). district groups facilities on the map and in the tables. target is the administrative number of children expected at that facility, and adds a column to the denominators panel.

Form paths (defaults are the eCHIS Niger SMC form)
How to read this

The buildings around each facility are fixed first (its nearest buildings, from the microplanner's own footprints), so the denominator never depends on where visits happened. A building counts as reached in a cycle when a household visited that cycle has its recorded GPS point within the chosen distance of it. Nothing here shows a household or a point.

The share depends on the distance and on how dense the buildings are: compare a district with itself over cycles, or districts at one distance, not districts of different density against each other.

Households with no usable point cannot be placed, so the share is a floor. Each is counted by kind under Data quality; none is dropped silently. A cycle's headline share is out of the buildings of the facilities that ran that cycle.
