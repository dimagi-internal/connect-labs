Messages from the frame

What just happened

  1. This system's server signed a statement: you ({{ request.user.username }}), this report, valid 60 seconds, once.
  2. It sent the statement to labs' /embed/token/. Labs checked the signature against this system's published keys (client {{ client_id }}), and that you may open the report.
  3. Labs answered with a one-time token. The frame traded it for a session for this one report, as you, read-only.
