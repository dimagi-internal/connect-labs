De-identified exports

An export applies the settings above to every visit in the window (no row limit), refuses to run if the visits carry a field the dictionary does not know, and checks its own output: rows against visits, a scan for names, usernames, phones, ids and GPS points from the source, value ranges, duplicates, visits with no case, and small groups of cases on fields that could single someone out. A manager other than the person who asked signs it off; it can then be downloaded for 7 days.
