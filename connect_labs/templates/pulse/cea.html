Every opportunity

Click a heading to sort. Modelled opportunities first, by rank.

Total cost is everything paid for the opportunity in USD: worker pay and the organisation's per-service fee on every completed work, plus startup and supply costs from Connect's custom invoices and costs entered in labs (cost data).

People reached counts distinct beneficiaries with approved work: each child, mother or baby once, however many workers, visits or payment units they passed through. Connect's beneficiary label (a name with a phone number or birth date, minus any visit suffix) is reduced to a keyed hash when Labs reads it, so people are counted without being stored. Only approved work counts: forms with no service delivered, and rejected, duplicate, over-limit or pending visits, never are. Work / person is approved work per person, a first look at how far people got through a longitudinal programme. An opportunity marked estimate has not been re-read since this count existed and uses its busiest payment unit instead.
