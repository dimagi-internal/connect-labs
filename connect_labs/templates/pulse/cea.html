How these numbers are made

Place. Each opportunity runs the model's own formulas once per area its approved visits fell in (by GPS): a district where Labs holds district figures, else the state or province. Each place input (diarrhoea deaths, ORS, vitamin A and deworming coverage, newborn and infant mortality, breastfeeding) takes the most local figure Labs holds, else the model's own figure for the country. The opportunity's model multiple is the visit-weighted mean across its areas. Click a multiple to see every step for that opportunity.

Total cost is everything paid for the opportunity in USD: worker pay and the organisation's per-service fee on every completed work, plus startup and supply costs from Connect's custom invoices and costs entered in labs (cost data). An opportunity that reached no one but was paid (an invoicing opportunity, for example) has its spend shared across the opportunities in the same programme, run by the same partner, that did reach people, by people reached, so every invoice counts once.

People reached counts distinct beneficiaries with approved work: each child, mother or baby once, however many workers, visits or payment units they passed through. Connect's beneficiary label (a name with a phone number or birth date, minus any visit suffix) is reduced to a keyed hash when Labs reads it, so people are counted without being stored. Only approved work counts: forms with no service delivered, and rejected, duplicate, over-limit or pending visits, never are. Work / person is approved work per person, a first look at how far people got through a longitudinal programme. An opportunity marked estimate has not been re-read since this count existed and uses its busiest payment unit instead.

Re-pricing. Each model's multiple is benefit per person over cost per person, with GiveWell's leveraging adjustment at 1, so multiple at actual cost = model multiple × model price ÷ actual cost per person.
